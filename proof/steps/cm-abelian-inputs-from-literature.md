## Statement

Let `O` be a term of [the structure OpenImageCondition](/step/open-image-condition-def/). Then the structure `LiteratureCMHypothesis` holds.

`LiteratureCMHypothesis` is a structure with 11 fields. Each field is a proposition of the namespace `GlobalTheory.Published`. This page does not unfold them. By their names, they state published results on abelian varieties, complex multiplication and functional equations:

1. `gm : GrothendieckDeformation`, deformations of abelian schemes.
2. `dr : AbelianDeRham`, de Rham cohomology of abelian schemes.
3. `dual : AbelianDualCrystal`, the crystal of the dual abelian scheme.
4. `per : PeriodComparison`, a comparison of periods.
5. `gmp : GaussManinPeriods`, periods and the Gauss-Manin connection.
6. `st : SerreTateGoodReductionSt`, the Serre-Tate theorem on good reduction.
7. `cm : EllipticMainTheoremCM`, the main theorem of complex multiplication for elliptic curves.
8. `hj : HilbertClassFieldJ`, the Hilbert class field generated by a $j$-invariant.
9. `dri : SiegelKlingenDeligneRibet`, the results of Siegel, Klingen and Deligne-Ribet on values of partial zeta functions.
10. `pz : PartialZetaFunctionalEquation`, the functional equation of partial zeta functions.
11. `fe : HeckeTateFunctionalEquation`, the functional equation of Hecke $L$-functions in the form of Tate.

## Proof

The proof fills each field with a theorem of the Lean code.

1. `gm` is `grothendieck_deformation_of_open O`.
2. `dr` is `abelian_de_rham_of_open O`.
3. `dual` is `abelianDualCrystal_of_dual_of_smoothLift` applied to `dual_abelian_scheme_of_open O` (a `DualAbelianScheme`) and to `crystallineSmoothLift_of_relations` (a `CrystallineSmoothLift`).
4. `per` is `periodComparison_holds`.
5. `gmp` is `GaussManinTop.main`.
6. `st` is `UpperTheory.Lit.serreTateGoodReduction`.
7. `cm` is `elliptic_main_theorem_cm_of_open O`.
8. `hj` is `hilbert_class_field_j_of_open O`.
9. `dri` is `siegel_klingen_deligne_ribet_of_open O`.
10. `pz` is `partial_zeta_functional_equation_of_open O`.
11. `fe` is `hecke_tate_functional_equation_of_open O`.

The fields `per`, `gmp` and `st`, and the second input of `dual`, do not take `O` at all.

## Notes

The hypothesis `O` is not used. Each theorem with the suffix `_of_open` takes `O` as an argument and ignores it. For example, `elliptic_main_theorem_cm_of_open O` is defined as `PositivityTheory.Prw.ellipticMainTheoremCM_holds`, and `hilbert_class_field_j_of_open O` is `GlobalTheory.Laid.hcfj_holds`. The two theorems that pass `O` on, `grothendieck_deformation_of_open` and `siegel_klingen_deligne_ribet_of_open`, pass it to other `_of_open` theorems that also ignore it. So the structure `LiteratureCMHypothesis` is proved with no hypothesis.

The [VWin chapter](/proof/vwin/) reports that the largest part of this sub-branch is `UpperTheory.Lit.serreTateGoodReduction`.
