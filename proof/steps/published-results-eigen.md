## Statement

Let $O$ be a proof of the structure [OpenLiteratureStatements](/step/open-literature-statements-def/). Then the proposition `GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses` holds.

`UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses` is a structure of type `Prop` with 60 fields. Each field is a proposition. Most field types are named statements of the namespace `GlobalTheory.Published`, for example `ClozelCohomological`, `GreenCuspidal`, `HeckeLAdicCharacter`, `ChebotarevDegreeOne`, `BaseChangeDefiniteUnitaryGroup` and `BhattScholzeCrystallineLattices`. Some fields have other types. Examples are `galoisRepOfPolarizedCuspidal : UpperTheory.GalS.CuspUAlt`, `localGlobalAboveL : UpperTheory.GalS.CarUAlt`, `litN : NonEndoscopicHypothesesAlt`, `toHypothesesX : GeometricGaloisRepCompatibility` and `nLink : GlobalTheory.NLink.Hypotheses₂`. The last three are bundles themselves. By their names, the fields are results on [[unitary groups|0R9R0Y1HDK]] and automorphic forms, on [[Galois representations|QW17YJ6ZCT]] attached to cusp forms, on local representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$, on the [[Weil representation|H3Q2703KKV]] and on Siegel-Weil formulas. This page does not unfold the 60 statements.

## Proof

The proof is a term in the `where` form. It fills each of the 60 fields with one theorem of the same file, applied to $O$. Each theorem has the type of its field. For example, `clozelCohomological` gets `clozel_cohomological_of_literature O`, `galoisRepOfPolarizedCuspidal` gets `cusp_u_of_literature O`, `localGlobalAboveL` gets `carU_above_l_of_literature O`, `litN` gets the bundle `literature_nonexistence O`, `toHypothesesX` gets the bundle `published_results_analytic O` and `nLink` gets the bundle `published_results_local O`. The field `siegelEisensteinSimplePole` gets `siegel_eisenstein_simple_pole O`, whose proof is `trivial`. Filling the fields is bookkeeping.

**Use of $O$.** Four of the 60 field theorems read a field of $O$, directly or through other theorems of the file. The other 56 do not.

1. `cusp_u_of_literature O` (field `galoisRepOfPolarizedCuspidal`) is `(bridgeStar_alt (gelfand_kazhdan_dual_of_literature O)).2 (pair_u_of_literature O)`. The theorem `pair_u_of_literature` applies `field_of_starFull_alt` to `local_langlands_recT_exists_of_literature O`, which is the field `O.localLanglandsExists`, and to the field `O.galoisRepOfPolarizedPairLit`.
2. `carU_above_l_of_literature O` (field `localGlobalAboveL`) applies `carU_of_finite_gl_recT` to `automorphic_forms_finite_gl_of_literature O`, to [local Langlands from the literature](/step/local-langlands-gl-rect-exists-of-literature/) and to `carU_local_global_of_literature O`. The second input is `O.localLanglandsExists`. The third reads `O.caraianiLocalGlobalDual`.
3. `hecke_l_adic_character_above_of_literature O` (field `heckeLAdicCharacterAbove`) uses `hecke_l_adic_character_crystalline_of_literature O`. That theorem uses `local_langlands_gl_recT_exists_of_literature O`, so it reads `O.localLanglandsExists`.
4. `literature_nonexistence O` (field `litN`) has five fields. Its field `loc` is `literature_local O`, whose field `llc` is `local_langlands_recT_exists_of_literature O`. Its field `aut` is `literature_automorphy O`, which reads `O.caraianiLocalGlobalDual` in its field `car` and uses `pair_u_of_literature O` and `hecke_l_adic_character_above_of_literature O`.

The two `Row` fields enter through `pair_u_of_literature` and `carU_local_global_of_literature`. Of the 195 theorems of the file, 13 read $O$, and this theorem is one of them.

The other 56 field theorems prove their statements with terms from other files. Some of them pass $O$ on to other theorems of the file, for example `doubling_method`, `flath_factorisation_of_literature`, `published_results_analytic` and `published_results_local`. None of these chains reaches a field of $O$.
