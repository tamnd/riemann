## For the overview

These concepts are enough to read [the proof in one page](/proof/).

1. [[Complex numbers|0CBAB9QQ9X]] and [[holomorphic functions|0DG0Z72CQD]].
2. [[Analytic continuation|0M3YQJ81NB]] and [[meromorphic functions|0NSD1TPHR0]].
3. The [[Gamma function|0V3FZMVB4K]] and its [[reflection formula|WF7KQAQTS2]].
4. The [[Riemann zeta function|TXB2F07CC6]], its [[Euler product|0C3PN4075R]] and its [[functional equation|NFXKNDJX9C]].
5. The [[Riemann hypothesis|VCGF9YR36X]] itself.
6. [[Dirichlet characters|JR4QSEFXWF]] and [[Dirichlet L-functions|24GAZHVTGB]].

## For the large part

The large part of the proof uses these subjects. Milestone M4 turns this list into paths with steps.

- [[Hecke characters|KPYFGB342X]] and the [[Dedekind zeta function|R3AR5JK8WW]].
- [[Automorphic forms|68SJQEC11F]] and [[the Langlands program|J9T2M084W5]].
- The [[theta correspondence|6WB6TKE70H]].
- [[Galois representations|QW17YJ6ZCT]] and the [[local Langlands conjectures|9E45NBX1G0]].

## For a reader who knows Lean

You can read the top of the proof with only Mathlib. Read `Statements.lean` and `Main.lean` first. See [the code page](/code/).
